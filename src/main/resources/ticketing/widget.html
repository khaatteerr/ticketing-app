The HelpDesk desktop app stays on your screen as a small floating icon. Hover over it whenever you need help.

  1. Get the HelpDesk Widget Windows ZIP from your IT team and extract the entire folder.
  2. Open HelpDesk Widget.exe and sign in with your work account. The default server is http://192.168.180.48:6080.
  3. Hover over the icon, choose an issue head and sub-issue, then click Submit ticket.
Open desktop widget ↗

Run the extracted app once before using this link. If nothing opens, start HelpDesk Widget.exe directly. Drag the icon to move it; right-click it to quit. Click the panel to keep it open, and use − or Escape to collapse it.
